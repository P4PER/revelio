Six slash commands, available in every channel the bot can see. Option names are
the same in every language - only their descriptions are translated, so you type
the same thing whether Discord is set to English or German.

## Card lookup

Looks up one card by name and posts it to the channel: text, flavour, cost,
lesson, rarity, legality, rulings and the art. Autocomplete suggests cards as you
type, and picking a suggestion sends the card's id, so exact spelling never
matters.

## Search

The same search the site runs, answered privately: only you see the results
until you decide to share some. Five cards to a page, each with its art, and
buttons to page through them. Lesson, card type and set can be changed on the
reply itself, without typing the command again, and the filters combine.
**Search** changes the search terms. Leave them out and the filters alone browse
a lesson, a type or a set.

Pick a card from the menu to open it in place, with everything `/card` shows
and a button back to the results. **Post to channel** puts that one card in the
channel. **Share** asks which cards of the page to post, all of them ticked to
start with; the channel gets them as a list that names you, with a menu anyone
can use to open one of the cards - privately, for whoever picked it.

## Deck lookup

Finds a deck by name and shows it as a picture of its cards - the same deck
sheet the builder's "Export PNG" downloads, grouped by card type with a copy
count on each card. The heading above it names the starting character, the
author, the format and legality. `view:list` answers with a text list instead,
which is easier to read on a slow connection and can be copied.

Start typing and Discord suggests matching decks: every public deck, plus your
own private decks if your account is linked, marked with a lock. Start with `@`
to search by author. A pasted deck link or id works too. If you send a name
without picking a suggestion and more than one deck matches, the bot asks you
privately which one you meant.

Your own private decks are never posted without asking. The bot first answers
only you, with **Post publicly** and **Only show me**; the buttons stop working
after a minute.

## Browse decks

Every public deck, privately, five to a page with the starting character's art,
most liked first. The menus on the reply change the format, the order and the
lesson. Give a deck name as `query`, or start it with `@` to search by author.
Open a deck to see it as a picture of its cards; **Post to channel** shares it,
naming you.

## Your own data

Two commands answer about you rather than about the game, so both reply
privately - only you see the result, even in a busy channel. Both need your
Discord account linked to Revelio first.

`/mydecks` lists five decks to a page, the way `/decks` does, with your private
ones marked with a lock, and opens any of them the same way. Posting one of your
private decks to the channel is your choice to make, with its button; nothing
else does it.
