Linking connects one Discord account to one Revelio account, so the bot knows
whose collection and whose decks to answer about. It is done on the website, not
in Discord, and it takes one click.

## What linking is for

Two of the five commands answer about you rather than about the game:
`/collection` and `/mydecks`. Those are the only ones that need a link. `/card`,
`/search` and `/deck` work for anyone in the server, linked or not.

There is no separate Revelio-to-Discord table behind this. The link *is* the
account record the sign-in system already keeps, tagged as a Discord one, which
is why unlinking removes it outright rather than flagging it inactive.

Signing in with Discord is not possible, on purpose. The connection is link-only:
you sign in to Revelio with an email code, and Discord is attached to that
account afterwards. A Discord profile on its own cannot create a Revelio account
or sign you into one.

## Linking your account

1. Sign in to Revelio the usual way, with a one-time code sent to your email.
2. Open **Settings**, then **Connections**.
3. Choose **Link Discord** and approve the prompt Discord shows you.

Revelio asks Discord for two things and no more: your identity - user id,
username and avatar - and your email address. It does not ask for your servers,
your friends, your messages, or anything you have written. Discord has to have
verified that email address; if it has not, the link is refused and Revelio says
so.

The two addresses do not have to match: the email on your Discord account and the
email on your Revelio account can be completely different, and linking still
works.

## Unlinking

The same Connections pane has **Unlink**. Using it does two things: it removes
the connection on Revelio's side, and it withdraws Revelio's authorization at
Discord, so Revelio is gone from your Discord *Authorized Apps* list as well
rather than lingering there unused.

Deleting your Revelio account does the same thing on the way out, so there is
nothing left to tidy up afterwards.

<Callout>
Once you unlink, `/collection` and `/mydecks` stop answering and reply with a link
back to Settings instead. Everything else keeps working. Linking again is the same
one click, and your collection and decks are untouched in the meantime - they live
on your Revelio account, not on the link.
</Callout>
